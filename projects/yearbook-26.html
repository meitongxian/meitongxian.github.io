Maggie Meitong Xian is a designer interested in systems of all sorts, with experiences spanning identity, editorial, and web. She holds a BFA in Graphic Design from Rhode Island School of Design, with minors in Computation and Sustainability. You’ll most likely find her near a body of water, but home will always be under the long white clouds of Aotearoa New Zealand.

Resume and PDF portfolio available upon request, reach out at maggiemxian@gmail.com.

RISD YEARBOOK 2026

Editorial, Creative Direction
Spring 2026

With Annabel Gillespie, Anushka Parikh, Francine Zhang, Kay Kim, Hyunmin Kim, Mary-Elizabeth Boatey, Neat Rodanant, Nadine Macapagal, Nicole Sun, Punch Kulphisanrat, Runping Mao Wei, Shiyan Zhu, Grace Zhao, Rachel Shin, & Coco Li @ RISD Design Guild.

The 2026 yearbook is a companion for wandering. Part field guide, part map still unfolding, this book follows the Class of 2026 through what has shaped their time at RISD. Like any good field guide, it offers a way to look closer: to notice what grows, what changes, and what leaves a trace. 🪨🌱

Edition of 450, printed and bound by Meridian Printing in East Greenwich, RI with a debossed cover and double ribbon bookmarks. Typeset in LL Bradford.
